Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the tradesense mobile application (the "App") handles information. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to the developer of the App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Summary

The App is designed so that it does not collect personal information. It requires no registration and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking technologies. We do not operate servers that receive or store user data.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or process personal information about users of the App. That includes names, email addresses, contact details, location data, device identifiers and financial account information.

3. Information stored on your device

Information you enter into the App is stored only in your device's local storage. This includes your watchlist, preferences, trade journal entries, price alerts and learning progress. We cannot access this information, and it is not sent to us. It is permanently removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

If you export data from the App (for example, your trade journal), the export file is saved to your device. Where you store or share that file is up to you.

4. Third-party data providers

To show market prices and charts, the App requests publicly available market data directly from these providers:

  • Crypto.com Exchange for cryptocurrency market data (Privacy Notice)
  • Yahoo Finance for equity market data (Privacy Policy)

These requests contain only the market symbols being looked up. As with any internet connection, the providers may receive technical information such as your device's IP address. No personal information is sent to them by the App. Their privacy policies, not this one, govern how they handle such information.

5. Notifications

Price alerts are evaluated and delivered on your device. They are not sent through any server we operate.

6. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. Because the App collects no personal information, we hold none for users of any age.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If the App's data practices change in any material way, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact tradesense.support@gmail.com.
